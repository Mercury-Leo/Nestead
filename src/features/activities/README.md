# activities
Things the family does together outside the kitchen. One page so far: Shows (`/shows`), the family's list of movies and series, looked up through `/api/shows` (OMDb today, chosen in `../../../server/shows/provider.ts`).

## How it works
- A show's details are saved as a row when it is added; the page reads rows through `useCollectionState(store.shows)`. Filters, sort and the name search run on those rows (`../../domain/shows.ts`), so opening the page or a card never calls `/api/shows`.
- `/api/shows` is called in four places only: Search in the add sheet (when the form is sent, never per keystroke), choosing a result (its details, short plot), Refresh details on one card, and Get their genres (`AddShow.tsx`, `actions.ts`). There is no refresh of the whole list, and nothing in the background.
- A result already on the list is marked "In your list"; choosing it, or saving one another device added a moment ago, opens the existing card instead of adding a second (`addShow()`; Supabase's unique constraint turns the second insert away). Opening a card clears filters that hide it, scrolls it into view, opens it, focuses its title and flashes it once (`reveal()` in `Shows.tsx`, `revealed` in `ShowCard.tsx`).
- Length is `runtimeMin`: a movie's running time, or one episode's for a series, shown beside its seasons (`Facts` in `ShowCard.tsx`).
- The status badge at the top of a card is also its control: a press cycles To watch, Watching, Watched; Watched stamps `watchedAt` and leaving it clears it (`statusPatch()`). The badge is drawn at the app's badge size inside a 44px target (`.statusHit`).
- Dropped is the fourth status, for a show the family decided not to watch. It is set on purpose with Drop it in the card's details (`dropShow()`), never by cycling past Watched. A dropped show's badge is muted, and its details offer Restore in Drop it's place (`restoreShow()`); either Restore or a press on the badge brings it back as To watch (`nextStatus()`). All, and its count, leave dropped shows out: they wait under the Dropped filter. Bringing a dropped show into view after Add show switches to that filter, since clearing the filters would not show it (`reveal()` in `Shows.tsx`).
- The star on a poster's top start corner (top left; top right in Hebrew) marks a family favourite, one they would watch again. It is a toggle for the whole family (`aria-pressed`, labelled with the title), apart from the status: starring never changes the status, and a status change, a drop or a refresh never changes the star (`toggleFavorite()`). The sort Favourites first lists starred shows first, each group newest added first (`sortShows()` in `../../domain/shows.ts`).
- Genres are saved with the rest of the details, in English as IMDb names them ("Sci-Fi"), and shown in the screen's language: `GENRE_KEY` in `labels.ts` maps IMDb's 28 genres to `shows.genre.*`, and a genre it does not know shows as stored. A card lists them on one line under the facts, cut short with an ellipsis.
- The Genres control (a button on desktop, a chip on phones, `ChipFilter.tsx`, with the theatre masks) opens a sheet of genre chips. A show must have every genre picked: Action and Comedy lists action comedies, and the control then reads "Action and Comedy" (`formatList()`). Each chip counts the listed shows that have that genre, which is what picking it would list, so only genres with a count are offered, plus those picked, which can always come off. Picking changes the list behind the sheet at once; Clear takes every genre off, and Show all and a reveal after Add show clear them too. The status counts follow the genres, as they follow the kind.
- The search matches each word against the title, the genres or the tags (`filterShows()` in `../../domain/shows.ts`), so "action comedy" finds action comedies and "batman action" an action show with Batman in its title. A genre is found by its English name and its name on screen (`genreName`, so "קומדיה" works in Hebrew), by the start of one of its words: "sci", "fi" and "scifi" find Sci-Fi, but "man" never finds Romance. A title is found by any part, as before.
- Tags are the family's own labels ("Bad movie", "Christmas"), shared by everyone and kept as typed, in any language, never translated. A tagged card shows them as chips under the genres (`Tags` in `ShowCard.tsx`); a card without tags has no row. Each chip is a button named "Show only shows tagged …": it adds its tag to the Tags filter and scrolls back up to the toolbar (`filterByTag` in `Shows.tsx`).
- Tags in a card's details opens `TagSheet.tsx`, rendered by the page: every tag the family uses, A to Z, pressed for this show's, and a field that narrows them or offers Add "…" (Enter does the same). Each press saves at once through `setTags()`; Done only closes. When the sheet closes (Done, Close, Esc or the backdrop), focus goes back to the Tags button that opened it, or to the page's search field when that button is gone (the show no longer matches the filters); `Sheet` gives focus back to its opener whenever it closes (`../../components/ui/Sheet.tsx`), and the page adds the fallback to the search field (`taggedFrom` in `Shows.tsx`). Add show closes the same way, so focus goes back to Add show. The Add chip puts focus back in the field, as Enter already leaves it, so the next tag can be typed at once. The "up to 20 tags" message sits in an always-present `role="status"` element, empty until the show is full, so a screen reader announces it; a full show lists every tag whatever is left in its disabled field. A typed tag is tidied and takes the family's spelling when one differs only in case (`tidyTag()`, `withTag()` in `../../domain/shows.ts`). A tag taken off its last show stays in the sheet until it closes. A show has at most 20 tags of up to 30 characters.
- The Tags control (`ChipFilter` with the tag icon, beside Genres) appears once any show has a tag, or while one is picked. A show must have every tag picked, whatever the case; the counts, Clear, Show all and a reveal after Add show work as they do for genres. The search finds a tag by the start of one of its words, as it finds a genre.
- Spellings that differ only in case are one tag everywhere (`sameTag()` in `../../domain/shows.ts`). The Tags filter names each tag the family's way, the spelling first met among all the shows (`familyTags()`), through `tagCounts(shown, family)` and `spelledAs(view.tags, family)` in `Shows.tsx`: a list narrowed to shows that spell a picked tag another way still names it as the family does, offers it pressed and counts it once. A chip pressed on a card counts a tag already picked in any spelling as picked (`sameTag` in `filterByTag`), so it is never picked twice. In the Tags sheet a chip is pressed, and a press takes off the show's own spelling, whatever the case.
- A show added before genres were saved has none (`genres` absent, `lacksGenres()`). While any such show is on the list, a band above the cards says how many and offers Get their genres (`GenreFill.tsx`): one lookup per show, three at a time, each saved as a refresh would (`fillGenres()` in `actions.ts`). A title the service no longer has saves an empty list, so it is not offered again. A run stops at the day's limit or when lookups are not set up, says why, and keeps offering the rest; the band goes away once every show has genres, on any device. A refresh that finds no genres also saves an empty list, so absent always means never read.
- Refresh writes `refreshPatch()`: the fetched fields and `fetchedAt`, never `status`, `watchedAt`, the star or who added it. A field the service no longer has is cleared. If the lookup fails, the card says so quietly and nothing is written. The server lets the browser keep an answer for ten minutes; a refresh asks with `cache: 'no-store'` (`lookupShow(id, true)`), so it never saves a cached copy under a new `fetchedAt`.
- Posters are the saved `posterUrl` (Amazon's image servers, as OMDb links them), loaded and cached by the browser with no referrer. Nothing downloads, uploads or stores an image. An Amazon poster link, `…/images/M/<id>._V1_<options>.jpg` (OMDb links a 380 px crop, `._V1_QL75_UX380_CR0,0,380,562_.jpg`, about 40 KB; older rows may hold `._V1_SX300.jpg`), gets a `srcset` of Amazon's `<id>._V1_QL75_SX100/200/300.jpg`, so a card (84–96 px wide) and a search result (44 px) fetch the size they draw: 139 KB instead of 361 KB for a phone's first nine posters. If that version fails, the poster tries the saved link, then shows the placeholder (`Poster.tsx`).
- A long list is built a page at a time: the first 60 of the filtered, sorted list (`SHOWS_PAGE`, `shownCount()` in `../../domain/shows.ts`), then Show more for the next 60, which moves focus to the first new card. A count under the list ("Showing 120 of 300", a polite live region) shows only when there is more than one page. Any change of search, filter or sort, back to an earlier one too, starts from one page. A show brought into view after Add show is built wherever it sorts (`Paging.reveal` in `Shows.tsx`). The list follows the name search through `useDeferredValue`, so the field paints before the cards are built.
- Long lists stay quick (measured in `../../../docs/PERFORMANCE.md#shows-2026-10-04-branch-activitiesshows`): a card builds its details panel only while open; cards are memoised on their fields, so a status change or a keystroke re-renders only the cards it touches; and cards out of view skip layout and paint (`content-visibility: auto`), each counted as its poster's height until laid out.
- The status, kind, genre and tag filters and the sort are a per-family device preference, `showsView` (`readPreference`/`writePreference`); the search is not kept. A view saved before genres or tags reads as none picked (`parseShowView()`).
- The Shows collection is not read at sign-in (`preloadStore()` leaves it out). A pointer over the Activities link, a press on it or focus starts the page's code and its first read (`../../app/warm.ts`), so the page usually opens with its rows; otherwise it shows Loading until its first read.

## Rules & gotchas
- Never call `/api/shows` to show the list or a card, and never refresh more than the one show someone asked for: OMDb's free key allows 1,000 requests a day for the whole app (`../../../server/shows/README.md`). The one bulk read is Get their genres, which someone presses, and which reads only shows without genres, once each (`fillGenres()`); keep it that way, never on page open.
- Save only `posterUrl`, never the image, and never an `img.omdbapi.com` link (it needs the key). `Poster.tsx` also refuses anything but https.
- Fetched text is data. Titles and plots render as React text; in a `<Trans>` string a title goes in a named slot (`<item/>` with `<bdi>`), never as a value (`shows.card.deleteQuestion`).
- Writes go through `shows/actions.ts`, and which fields a refresh may write is decided in `../../domain/shows.ts` (`REFRESHED_FIELDS`), not in a screen.
- Stored statuses are kebab-case (`to-watch`); translation keys are camelCase (`shows.status.toWatch`), mapped by `STATUS_KEY` in `labels.ts`. Genres are stored as IMDb names them (`Sci-Fi`, `Film-Noir`) and mapped by `GENRE_KEY` (`shows.genre.sciFi`): a genre IMDb adds later needs a line there and in both locale files, or it shows in English.
- Genre filters hold the stored English names, never translated ones, so a view saved in one language works in the other.
- Write tags as a whole list through `setTags()`; removing the last writes `[]`, never `undefined` (the column is not null). Tags render as typed, inside `<bdi>` on a card and in the Tags sheet's chips; the tag filter holds them as typed too. Where a tag sits inside a composed string it goes in a `<Trans>` slot with a `<bdi>`, never as a value: each name in the filter sheet (`<name/>` in `shows.filter.option`, genres too) and the Add chip's tag (`<item/>` in `shows.tags.add`), so a Hebrew tag in an English sentence, or the reverse, cannot reorder its neighbours.
- With genres or tags picked, the filter's chip carries a screen-reader label ("Genres: Action and Comedy", `.visually-hidden`, absolutely positioned). The chip must stay its containing block (`position: relative` in `ChipFilter.module.css`): without it the label escaped the phone's scrolling chip row and widened the page, and the open sheet and the tab bar were laid out off screen (`../../components/README.md`).
- A chip row's items must keep their width limits, or a 30-character tag runs past the card or widens the page on a phone (the same class of bug as the Genres chip fixed in 8c4516c). On a card: `.tags > li { min-width: 0; max-width: 100% }` in `ShowCard.module.css`, with the tag's own `max-width` and its `<bdi>` ending in an ellipsis. In the Tags sheet: `.options > button` and its last child in `TagSheet.module.css`. In the filter sheet's chips: the same `max-width` in `ChipFilter.module.css`, with each chip's text in a `<span>` that lays the name (`.name`, a `<bdi>`) and the count side by side (`display: inline-flex`), so only the name ends in an ellipsis and the count stays visible. A long tag ends in an ellipsis in all three.
- Shows UI stays in this folder; it imports nothing from `../larder/`.
- `ShowCard`'s memo compares `show` field by field (`sameShow()`), because a re-read returns every row as a new object; `genres` and `tags`, the two lists, are compared item by item. The memo also compares `onTag` and `onEditTags`: the page must keep them the same functions between renders (`useCallback`), or every card re-renders on every keystroke. A new prop for the card must join that comparison, or the card ignores changes to it, and a new list or object field needs its own comparison there, or every card re-renders on every read.
- `contain-intrinsic-size` in `ShowCard.module.css` is the closed card's content height, padding and border excluded: the poster's height at 84 px wide (126 px) and at 96 px on desktop (144 px). If the poster or the card's padding changes, change it too, or the scrollbar jumps as cards appear. The genres line keeps to one line for the same reason; a card with a two-line title, a wrapped facts line and genres runs a little past the poster.
- The star follows the title and the status badge in the DOM, so someone moving by headings meets it after the show's name; CSS places it over the poster against the card (`.starHit`), from the card's 12px padding. If that padding or the poster's corner moves, change its offsets too. Read the field as `favorite === true` and unstar by writing `false` (`../../data/README.md`).
- The details panel is built only while open, so closing a card resets a pending delete question and a refresh message.
- Paging state is keyed to the search, filters and sort (`keyOf()` in `Shows.tsx`) and reset during render when the key changes; the key uses the search as typed, while the list uses the deferred one, so a reveal set in the same event survives. The browser's find-in-page only finds cards already built: past the first 60, people use the name search or Show more.
- After Show more, focus moves to the first new card's title, found by its position in the grid (`grid.current.children[n]`): keep one card per child there.
- The Tags sheet is rendered by the page, not inside the card: the grid must keep one card per child, and cards sit under `content-visibility: auto`.
- `Poster`'s `sizes` must match the width the CSS draws it at (`ShowCard.module.css`, `AddShow.module.css`), or the browser fetches the wrong size.
